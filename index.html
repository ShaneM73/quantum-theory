Quantum Theory Labs

From quaternions to quantum entanglement

A path that starts from 3Blue1Brown's quaternion video. First see how four-dimensional numbers are projected into 3D, then find that a qubit lives on the same hypersphere, that quantum gates are quaternion rotations, and finally use two qubits to explore entanglement and Bell's inequality. Every step is hands-on: drag, rotate, measure.

Quaternion sphereQuaternions · stereographic projection

Multiply the unit sphere by q and watch it grow, flatten into a plane and turn back. See the difference between 180° and 360°, and why q p q⁻¹ rotates by 2θ.

Open 2

Hopf fibers and the Bloch sphereHopf fibration · Bloch sphere

A qubit (a, b) is a point on the unit sphere in 4D. The global phase moves it around a loop, and that whole loop is a single point on the Bloch sphere: 3 − 1 = 2 dimensions.

Open 3

Gates are rotationsGates · SU(2) · spin-½

X, Y, Z, H, S and T are all rotations of the Bloch sphere, each matching a unit quaternion. Turn to 360° to see the minus sign; only 720° truly restores the state.

Open 4

EntanglementH + CNOT · measurement · CHSH

Build a Bell state step by step and watch both arrows shrink into the centre. Choose measurement directions, collect correlations, and check S = 2√2 > 2 yourself.
