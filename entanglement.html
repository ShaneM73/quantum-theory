1Why H and CNOT entangle

HA turns |00⟩ into (|00⟩ + |10⟩)/√2. B is still 0, so nothing is entangled yet. CNOT then acts on each term separately: |00⟩ stays, |10⟩ becomes |11⟩. The result is (|00⟩ + |11⟩)/√2. CNOT copies the value of A onto B, while A itself is both 0 and 1.

2Why the arrows shrink

The Bell state as a whole is completely definite, but A or B alone gives 0 and 1 half the time each, with no preferred direction, so its arrow shrinks to a point at the centre. Drag the β slider to watch it shrink gradually.

3Measurement correlations

With the same direction on both sides, the Bell state always gives the same result. With directions differing by Δ, the correlation is E = cos Δ. After one measurement both arrows jump to definite positions and the entanglement is gone.

4Bell's inequality

If the results were written down in advance (local hidden variables), S = E(a,b) − E(a,b′) + E(a′,b) + E(a′,b′) could not exceed 2. Quantum mechanics reaches 2√2 ≈ 2.83.
