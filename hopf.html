1"Physically the same state"

Press Play to turn γ. The values of a and b keep changing, but |a|² and |b|² do not move at all. Measurements only see these probabilities, so all these (a, b) are the same physical state, and the point on the right stays still.

2Why it is a circle

As γ goes from 0° to 360°, the pink point on the left travels once around the coloured circle and returns. Every point on that loop is the same physical state.

3A loop becomes a point

4Why the sphere is 2-dimensional

"Dimension" means how many numbers you need to locate a point. On the Bloch sphere two are enough, latitude and longitude; on the left you need γ as well. Squeezing each loop to a point removes one number: 3 − 1 = 2.

5Relative phase matters

Only the phase that turns a and b together is meaningless. The phase difference φ between them is the longitude: |0⟩ + |1⟩ and |0⟩ − |1⟩ are opposite points on the equator and are different states.
